Schools in New Obour

School options in Obour and New Obour: government, language, international, and Nile schools. Addresses, fees where published, and practical questions before applying.

For families with children, the school decision often comes before the home decision. Obour and New Obour offer government schools, language schools, international schools (British/American), and Nile schools, but the mix is not spread evenly across districts.

Types of schools in the directory

  • Government schools: widely available and lowest cost; follow the Egyptian national curriculum.
  • Language schools: Egyptian national curriculum with some subjects taught in English.
  • International schools: follow foreign curricula such as IGCSE; fees are higher and admissions open earlier.
  • Nile schools: Egyptian-Japanese partnership schools with a separate admissions portal.

Published individual pages

  • Nile Egyptian School — Obour (Arabic)
  • International Public School (IPS) — Obour (Arabic)
  • Egyptian-Japanese School — Obour (Arabic)
  • St. Joseph School — Obour (Arabic)

Before you apply

  1. Confirm the curriculum and accreditation.
  2. Ask for the current fee schedule and any refundable deposits.
  3. Check transport options and pickup timing.
  4. Ask about waiting lists, especially for international grades.
