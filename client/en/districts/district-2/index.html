District 2 in New Obour

Guide to District 2 in New Obour: execution phase, service status, who it suits, and how to evaluate the district yourself before a site visit.

District names in a new city are not enough on their own. One district can contain plots at very different stages, and the same street can differ in paving, lighting, and services. The right comparison starts with your time goal: move-in within months, early purchase with delayed utility completion, or medium-horizon investment?

How to evaluate this district yourself

  1. Street readiness: Are paving, lighting, and sidewalks in place on the specific street, not only at the gate?
  2. Core utilities: Are water, electricity, and sewer connected to the advertised plot or building?
  3. Execution timing: If buying early, what is the written date for utility delivery, not just units?
  4. Entry point: How do you reach the specific street from the main axis during rush hour?
  5. Neighboring uses: What is planned next to the site and will it affect quietness or orientation?
