Apartments for Sale in Obour City

How to buy an apartment in Obour City: where to search, how prices really vary by district, resale vs developer vs government projects, and the inspection checklist before you sign.

Searching for an apartment in Obour means choosing between three very different markets: resale units in established districts, developer compounds, and government housing projects. This guide maps each one, explains how prices actually vary across the city, and gives you the checklist that protects you before signing anything.

Editorial note: asking prices move monthly in Egypt's current market. Treat any figure you read — here or anywhere — as a snapshot, and verify current levels on our prices page and through active agents before negotiating.

The three markets you are actually searching

MarketWhat you getWatch out for
Resale in established districtsImmediate move-in, real neighborhood life, negotiable pricesBuilding age and documentation — inspect hard, verify titles
Developer compoundsPayment plans, new finishes, managed communitiesTotal installment cost vs cash price; delivery timelines
Government projects (Dar Misr, Sakan Misr)State-backed, finished units, structured allocationApplication windows and eligibility rules per offering

For compound options see our compounds guide, and for the step-by-step legal process use the buying guide.

How prices really vary across Obour

Three factors move the per-meter price more than anything else. First, district maturity: established numbered districts with working services price differently from newer expansions — compare areas in the districts guide. Second, building condition: in older districts, two adjacent buildings can differ more than two districts. Third, legal cleanliness: a unit with a flawless registered chain commands a real premium, and one without it deserves a real discount.

Where to search

  • Local agents — still the deepest inventory for resale; work with two or three active real estate offices (Arabic directory) rather than one.
  • Online portals — useful for price discovery, less for accuracy; treat listings as leads, verify by phone and visit.
  • Developer sales offices — the only reliable source for new-project terms; always get the payment schedule in writing.
  • Official announcements — government project offerings are published through official channels only; ignore broker posts about them.

The pre-signing checklist

  1. Ownership chain and building permit — verified, not shown on a phone screen.
  2. Engineering inspection for older units: roof, plumbing, electrics, humidity.
  3. Five comparable listings collected before your first offer.
  4. Evening visit to the street — the neighborhood at 9 PM is the one you will live in.
  5. Monthly cost reality: maintenance fees, and your actual commute via the transport guide.

Bottom line

The Obour apartment market rewards methodical buyers: pick your district by daily life, verify documents before price talks, and never compare units on headline price alone. Start with the current price benchmarks, then let the checklist — not the listing agent — drive your decision.

Frequently asked questions

How much do apartments cost in Obour City?

Prices vary widely by district maturity, building condition, and legal status, and they move monthly in the current market. Check our prices page for current benchmarks and collect five comparable live listings before negotiating — any static figure ages quickly.

Is it better to buy resale or from a developer in Obour?

Resale gives immediate occupancy and negotiable prices but needs harder inspection and title verification. Developer units offer payment plans and new finishes but cost more in total than the advertised installment suggests. Compare the full cost of both against your timeline.

What documents must I verify before buying in Obour?

The registered ownership chain first, then the building permit, and confirmation there are no recorded violations or disputes. Utility meters in the seller's name are a useful cross-check. For significant amounts, a property lawyer's review is cheap insurance.

Which district in Obour is best to buy in?

There is no universal best — the right district depends on your commute, school needs, and budget. Compare areas using the districts guide with the same criteria, visit your shortlist in the evening, and let daily-life fit decide before price does.
